# InstaReelLimit 🚫📱  
A small Chrome extension that helps you control your Instagram scrolling habit.  
It counts how many Reels you watch and blocks the Reels page once you hit your limit — simple, effective, and good for your focus.

---

## 💡 Why I built this
I noticed I was spending way too much time watching Reels without realizing it.  
So I made a little tool that actually keeps me accountable.  
Now, when I reach my limit, the Reels tab just stops working. Peace restored. 😅

---

## ⚙️ Features

- ⏱️ Counts the number of Reels you watch  
- 🚫 Automatically blocks the Reels page after your set limit  
- 🔔 Optional notification before blocking  
- 💾 Saves your daily count locally  
- 🔄 Resets every 24 hours  

---

## 🧭 How it works
The extension runs in the background while you use Instagram on desktop.  
Once you scroll through a certain number of Reels, it hides or blocks the Reels section for the rest of the day.  
You can adjust your limit in the settings panel.
